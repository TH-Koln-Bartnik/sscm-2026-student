Footprint widget · amount × factor

Footprint (kg CO₂e) = Σ amount × emission factor

Footprint per unit = total footprint ÷ units (the “functional unit”: one guest, one bag of chips, one use).

Share of a stage = stage footprint ÷ total footprint × 100 %. The biggest stage is the hotspot.

CO₂e = CO₂ equivalent: all greenhouse gases counted as if they were CO₂. Amount in the factor’s unit: kWh, litres, kg, tonne-km.

Reuse break-even (uses) = extra footprint to make the reusable item ÷ footprint saved per use. Round up.

Footprint saved per use = footprint of the single-use items one use replaces − washing per use.

Extra footprint to make = what the reusable item “costs” in CO₂e before its first use. Footprint to make = mass × factor.

Life cycle cost = purchase price + fuel cost per km × km per year × years + other operating cost per km × km per year × years

Crossover (km) = (price B − price A) ÷ (variable cost per km A − variable cost per km B); in years = crossover km ÷ km per year.

GHG break-even (km) = extra GHG to build B ÷ GHG saved per km.

Variable cost per km = energy (fuel or electricity) cost per km + other operating cost per km. Life cycle cost: Heizer et al. (2023), Supplement 5, Equation S5-2.

Cost per tonne avoided (€/t CO₂e) = extra cost per year ÷ tonnes CO₂e saved per year. Negative = the measure saves money.

Cheapest-first plan: sort the measures by € per tonne, add them from the cheapest until the target is reached. The picture is a cost curve (“marginal abatement cost curve”): bar width = tonnes saved, bar height = € per tonne.

Compare with the carbon price: EU ETS allowance 82.40 € per tonne (5 Oct 2026).
