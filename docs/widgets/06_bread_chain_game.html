Bread chain game

Session 6 · a beer game in the browser · works on a phone · about 8 minutes

The chain: customers → Store → Bakery → Mill → Farm → field. Goods travel down the chain, orders travel up.

Your job: each round, decide how many cases you order from your supplier. Keep your cost low. The other three stages are played by the computer.

Cost per round: 0.50 € for every case in stock + 1.00 € for every case you still owe your customer (backlog).

Delays: your order reaches your supplier after 1 round; goods take 2 more rounds to reach you. You start with 12 cases in stock. 24 rounds.
